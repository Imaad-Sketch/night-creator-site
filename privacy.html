Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

1. Information NIGHT may access

NIGHT Creator may access basic account information and videos that the user specifically selects for uploading. It may also receive access and refresh tokens after the user approves a platform connection.

2. How information is used

Information is used only to provide requested creator features, such as connecting an account, checking a selected video and sending that video to the user's TikTok drafts.

3. Local storage

NIGHT Creator is designed as a local-first desktop application. Credentials and creator records are intended to remain on the user's device rather than being stored on a public NIGHT server.

4. Sharing and selling

NIGHT Creator does not sell personal information. Information is only shared with connected services when required to perform an action approved by the user.

5. Content publishing

NIGHT Creator does not publish public content without the user's clear approval. Draft uploads are completed and reviewed through the official TikTok application.

6. Data deletion

Users can disconnect their account and remove locally stored credentials and creator records from the NIGHT application.

7. Security

Reasonable local security measures are used, but no computer system can guarantee complete security.
